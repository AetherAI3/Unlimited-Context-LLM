## Interpretation

The sections above are generated by `python -m bench.latent_transfer.run report`. This section was
written by hand after the run.

### Verdict

The proposed verdict is **SUPPORTED** for G-W7 content dependence on unseen documents, unseen names and
unseen question phrasings. The extension to the unseen yes/no task family is **not supported**. The
lane proposes the verdict; the orchestrator owns the gate.

### What the controls establish

- **H1 holds with a wide margin.** On unseen phrasings the paired capsule scores 0.547 against
  0.056–0.113 for blank, shuffled, mismatched and no capsule. Every paired-minus-control lower
  95% bound is between 0.409 and 0.480, against a preregistered margin of 0.10.
- **The answers follow the capsule's content.** With a capsule from another document (shuffled) or
  with the same name and changed facts (mismatched), the receiver gives the answer the capsule's
  source document dictates 0.56 and 0.55 of the time. That matches the paired accuracy (0.547).
  So the receiver reads facts from the capsule, not the person's identity or the question.
- **The transfer is partial.** Paired accuracy by attribute:

  | Attribute | Paired | No capsule |
  |---|---|---|
  | job | 0.965 | 0.10 |
  | pet | 0.99 | 0.115 |
  | city | 0.11 | 0.125 |
  | colour | 0.125 | 0.10 |

  The trained projector learned to carry two of the four facts nearly perfectly and the other two
  not at all. The 0.547 mean averages those two regimes; it does not describe a uniformly weak
  channel. Why city and colour were dropped is not established. Possible causes are capacity or
  allocation in 8 slots × single-head attention, or the training budget, which was stopped while
  the loss was still falling.
- **No transfer to the unseen yes/no task family (H2 fails).** Paired is 0.496, the controls are
  0.487–0.511 and every CI spans 0. In every capsule arm and with no capsule the receiver answers
  "Yes" 85–89% of the time; only blank (46%) and text context (50%) differ. Because each document
  has exactly two true and two false claims, a constant answer scores 0.5. The projector was
  trained only on attribute-recall answers, and the frozen receiver does not reuse the injected
  facts to verify a claim.
- **The exact anchor stays authoritative.** With the anchor text in the prompt the receiver
  scores 0.995 and 0.999, so the capsule is far below the exact record (−0.448 on unseen
  phrasings). Every capsule carries the anchor digest, and all 200 anchors verified. Anything
  that must be exact has to come from the anchor.

### Limits

- Single training seed, one small model (0.5B), CPU only.
- Synthetic single-person records with 4 attributes × 8 values. Generalisation to real documents,
  longer contexts or other models is not tested.
- First-token scoring over a closed candidate set, not free generation.
- The receiver loads the projector file only to match the adapter id; it never runs it.
- Grant authenticity is out of scope: grants are unsigned manifests (see
  `docs/LATENT_HANDOFF.md`).
